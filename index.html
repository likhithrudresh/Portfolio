About Me

Get to know more about my background and what drives me

I'm an early-career quality and operations engineer with an MSc in Quality and Operations Management from Chalmers University of Technology and a BEng in Mechanical Engineering. I enjoy finding where a process loses time or quality, measuring it properly, and making the improvement stick.

In a Lean Six Sigma student project with Cepheid in Gothenburg, I applied DMAIC to equipment validation in a regulated manufacturing environment. The project reduced lead time from 20.5 to 9 weeks and cut workload by 67%. For my master's thesis at RISE Research Institutes of Sweden, I led an FMEA-based risk analysis that became the basis for a continuous improvement governance framework adopted by the engineering teams.

Before Chalmers, I interned at Thermax in Bengaluru, working with quality inspections, root cause analysis and supplier performance dashboards. Alongside my studies, I led the student mentor programme at the Chalmers International Reception Committee, onboarding more than 1,000 international students.
